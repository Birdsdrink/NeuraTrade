Color Tokens

Base colors
- Primary Background: #050505
- Secondary Background: #0B0B0D
- Card Background: #111214
- Elevated Card: #17181B
- Subtle Border: rgba(255,255,255,0.07)
- Primary Text: #FFFFFF
- Secondary Text: #A1A1AA
- Muted Text: #71717A

Accent palette (use sparingly)
- Purple: #7C3AED
- Blue: #3B82F6
- Cyan: #06B6D4
- Green: #22C55E
- Yellow: #FACC15
- Orange: #F59E0B
- Red: #EF4444
- Pink: #EC4899

Usage guidance
- Keep backgrounds near-black; cards in dark charcoal.
- Use accents for status, indicators, and call-to-actions only.
- Avoid multiple accent colors on the same small component.
- Apply subtle semi-transparent borders to distinguish cards.
